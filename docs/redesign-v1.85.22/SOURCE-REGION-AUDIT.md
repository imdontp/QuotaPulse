## Production boundaries

- Live uses observed sessions, source counts, call-grain charts and recorded feed.
  Absent minute records must not be filled with invented activity.
- Providers uses supported detected/catalogued owners and reader freshness;
  unavailable integrations and connection latency must not be invented.
- Cost uses observed native/API monetary basis and known historical savings.
  Hypothetical savings, invoices and unsupported projections remain unavailable.
- Alerts uses one owner/window's observations and valid forecast. The 50/80/95
  rules remain read-only and recommendations do not execute workload actions.

These substitutions do not exempt geometry, illustration treatment, typography
or panel composition from source alignment. The reviewed eight-page baseline
and explicit visual acceptance remain outstanding.
